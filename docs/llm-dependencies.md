Special notice obligations to preserve during packaging:

- `ring`: retain its bundled LICENSE, including its Apache-2.0 and ISC notices.
- `webpki-roots`: retain the CDLA-Permissive-2.0 license with the root data.
- `libdbus-sys`: the Linux vendored D-Bus source has its own COPYING and
  copyright notices, separate from the Rust wrapper's MIT/Apache license.
  That COPYING offers AFL-2.1 or GPL-2.0-or-later for the library. Preserve
  it and the corresponding vendored source alongside distribution notices.
- All MIT components require their copyright/permission notices; Apache-2.0
  components require the license and any applicable NOTICE material. BSD and
  ISC alternatives likewise require retention of their upstream notices.
